# Development Report

**Date:** 06 May 2026
**Prepared by:** Islem

---

## Work Completed

### 1. Publeader — Advertiser Profile & Brand Identity

- Advertisers can now manage their company profile end-to-end from the web dashboard: legal information (legal name, SIRET, VAT number, legal form), contact details, sector, headquarters, and website.
- Built brand-identity controls: each company picks a brand color and uploads a brand logo (signed direct upload to Cloudinary, replaces previous logo automatically).
- Brand color and logo now propagate everywhere campaigns appear: driver-facing campaign cards in the mobile app show a colored accent strip and the real brand logo, replacing the previous generic favicons.
- Mobile advertiser profile screen rebuilt against live data: live legal info section, real company logo with brand color tint, pull-to-refresh.

---

### 2. Publeader — Team Management

- Advertisers can now invite teammates by email with three role levels: Admin (full access), Editor (campaigns + assets only), Viewer (read-only).
- Built the invitation flow end-to-end: email is sent automatically with an accept link, invitee clicks the link, accepts the invitation, lands on the dashboard with the right permissions applied.
- The accept page handles every edge case cleanly: invitation expired, already accepted, wrong email logged in, no account yet.
- Web team page wired with live data: members list with last-seen activity, pending invitations panel with resend/cancel actions, role change dropdown, member removal — with safety guards (you can't demote or remove the last admin, you can't modify yourself).
- Standard role-permission matrix enforced on the server, so even if someone bypasses the UI they can't escalate privileges.

---

### 3. Publeader — Asset Library

- Advertisers can now build a creative library for their brand: upload visuals, videos, logos, and briefs (PDF/DOCX), with per-type size limits (images 10 MB, logos 5 MB, briefs 20 MB, videos 100 MB).
- Built the upload flow with Cloudinary auto-detection: a single upload modal handles images, videos, and document files with the right preview for each — image thumbnails inline, video posters auto-generated by Cloudinary, briefs as document tiles.
- Library page wired with live data: type filter (all / visuals / videos / logos / briefs), search, per-asset usage count (number of campaigns referencing it), open and delete actions.
- Delete is blocked when an asset is in use by a campaign — the advertiser must remove it from the campaigns first, with a clear French error message.

---

### 4. Publeader — Campaign Creation Wizard

- Built a complete 3-step campaign creation wizard on the web: Brief → Targeting → Budget.
  - **Brief:** name, type (Flocage vehicle wraps OR Borne kiosk ads), brand, description, asset selection from the library.
  - **Targeting:** city, zones, dates, capacity (number of drivers for Flocage, number of bornes + impressions target for Borne).
  - **Budget:** tier picker (BOOST / GROWTH / LEADER) with one-click presets, total budget, per-driver reward.
- Tier picker auto-fills sane defaults (budget, duration, capacity) but every value remains overridable for ad-hoc campaigns.
- Two clear save paths: "Save as draft" keeps the campaign editable, "Publish" goes live immediately (status auto-decided based on start date — upcoming or active).
- Edit-after-publish handled correctly: drafts are fully editable, but once published the commercial commitments are frozen (type, city, start date, budget, tier, reward, capacity); only description, end date, zones, and assets remain editable.
- Campaigns list rebuilt with live data, status filter chips (draft / upcoming / active / completed), and direct edit/detail links.

---

### 5. Publeader — Campaign Detail & Driver Assignment

- Built the advertiser campaign detail page on the web with three tabs: Overview (brief + computed metrics), Drivers/Terminals (capacity management), Assets (linked previews).
- Advertisers can now assign and unassign drivers manually to Flocage campaigns from the dashboard:
  - Eligible drivers list shows only validated drivers in the right city who are not currently busy on another campaign.
  - Atomic capacity check on the server prevents overbooking (two assignments racing to claim the last slot will only succeed once).
  - Drivers can still self-accept campaigns from mobile as before — both paths feed the same assignment list.
- Borne campaigns get terminal assignment: advertisers add and remove terminal IDs up to the campaign's borne count (real terminal validation will plug in once partner terminals ship).
- Computed metrics shown live on the overview tab: period consumed %, days remaining, driver fill %, kilometres %, no fake numbers.
- Mobile advertiser campaigns screen rewired against live data: list with status filter, pull-to-refresh, modal detail showing brief, dates, budget, capacity, and the assigned drivers (or terminals for Borne campaigns) — read-only as designed.

- On-site at the office, brainstorming each section before implementation, validating tradeoffs (frozen-after-publish fields, last-admin guard, single-city per campaign, role permission matrix), and reviewing progress with the team.

## Summary

Today the platform moved from "core driver experience done" to "advertisers can run their own campaigns end-to-end on the web". An advertiser can now log in, set up their brand identity, invite teammates with proper role-based permissions, build a library of creative assets, create a campaign through a 3-step wizard with two diffusion types and three budget tiers, save as draft or publish, then manage assigned drivers (or terminals) on a live detail page. Five major sections of the roadmap (A1 to A5) shipped today across both backend, web dashboard, and mobile read-only views.
